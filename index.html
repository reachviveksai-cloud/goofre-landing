01 · Why now

The two-front squeeze, and the way up.

Front one: the website is losing its shoppers. Agents crawl your catalog, compare it against every competitor, and answer inside ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude. The shopper never sees the theme, the UX, or the checkout you built. The agent does.

Front two: the platforms are automating the rest. Every release from Shopify and its peers absorbs another task that used to be a project: themes, setup, integrations. That is not a complaint. Automating is their job. But it thins the work that made e‑commerce development a career.

The work that remains cannot be platform-automated, because it sits above the platforms. Making stores legible, verifiable and transactable to agents is protocol work, the same across Shopify, Magento and WooCommerce, and it never finishes: the standards move monthly. When servers went to the cloud, the sysadmins who moved up the stack became DevOps engineers and ran the industry. This is that moment for e‑commerce developers.

The role isn't disappearing. It's being promoted.

04 · The platform you operate

One platform. Every moving part of agent readiness.

Readiness is not a plugin install. It is precision work across storefront markup, product feeds, identity, compliance and checkout: many interdependent systems where one wrong value means the store looks fine and fails silently. It is closer to a surgeon's work than to setup. Goofre™ is the operating theatre: the instruments, the monitoring and the discipline in one place, operated by the ACO™. It keeps a store conformant to the standards agents actually use — ACP for checkout, AP2 for payment, MCP for access — not one vendor's plugin.

Built to be argued with

The engine tests whether an agent could actually act on what a store publishes, not whether a tag is merely present, which is why its scores run tougher than free checkers. Every finding names where the problem lives and a fix that matches its cause, and every audit records its engine version so month-over-month trajectory is honest.

Where this goes

Readiness — conformance to the standards agents use (ACP, AP2, MCP), scored by Goofre™'s engine — is the first thing ACO™s orchestrate. It will not be the last: the ambition is an orchestration layer for everything commerce needs in the agent era.
